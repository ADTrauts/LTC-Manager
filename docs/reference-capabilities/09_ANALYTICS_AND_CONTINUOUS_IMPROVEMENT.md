# Capability: Analytics and Continuous Improvement

**Capability ID:** CAP-09  
**Parent:** [00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md](./00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md)

---

## Purpose

**Learn from completed operations** — measure performance, reliability, and trends — to improve how the organization executes over weeks and months.

Analytics looks **backward and across** to strengthen **forward execution**. It serves improvement, not opening glance.

---

## Operational promise

Leadership can see **what keeps failing**, **what keeps saving the day**, **where to invest training and capital**, and **whether the organization is becoming more reliable** — without confusing historical study with live awareness.

---

## Inputs

- **Completed operations** — outcomes, partial success, failures.
- **Readiness history** — blocked frequency, time-to-ready.
- **Workforce data** — call-off patterns, chronic gaps, override frequency.
- **Issue history** — recurrence, time-to-resolve, recovery types.
- **Asset reliability** — MTBF, repeat failures, PM compliance.
- **Supply history** — chronic shorts, delivery performance.
- **Compliance outcomes** — failures, near-misses tied to operations.
- **Survey and audit results** (external) — correlated to operational data where possible.

---

## Outputs

### Performance

Operation success rate — on time, complete, safe — by type, location, shift.

### Reliability

Trend toward **fewer surprises** — repeat issues declining, readiness improving.

### Operational trends

Seasonal, day-of-week, shift patterns — "Tuesdays after holiday."

### Recurring issues

Same warmer, same servery, same handoff delay — prioritized for fix.

### Continuous improvement initiatives

Tracked actions — training, capital, process change — linked to evidence.

### Benchmarking (maturity)

Compare sites within operator — anonymous peer norms in industry platform.

### Survey readiness

Leading indicators — chronic cleanliness gaps, repeat temp failures before survey season.

### Leadership reporting

Concise narratives for executives — outcomes, risk, investment case — not raw tables only.

## Distinctions

| Analytics | Operational Intelligence |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Completed operations | Active operations |
| Trends and patterns | Current state |
| Weekly/monthly review | 15-second open |
| Improvement investments | Walk order now |
| Accountability | Action |

Both are necessary. **Never swap them on open.**

## Operational rules

1. **Outcome metrics first** — service delivered, not forms filed.
2. **Recurring beats anecdote** — three warmer failures > one bad day story.
3. **Improvement closes loop** — insight → action → remeasure.
4. **No floor shame boards** — analytics for leaders, not punitive wall charts on servery.
5. **Privacy and labor sensitivity** — workforce analytics respect policy — industry pack.
6. **Survey linkage** — correlate, don't claim causation without care.

## Success criteria

| Criterion | Measure |
|-----------|---------|
| **Actionable insights** | Reports lead to named improvements |
| **Recurrence reduction** | Top issues trend down after intervention |
| **Reliability gain** | Readiness blocked time decreases |
| **Leadership clarity** | Executives understand operational performance |
| **Honest variance** | Partial success in data, not binary gaming |
