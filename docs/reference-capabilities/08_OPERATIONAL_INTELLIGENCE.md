# Capability: Operational Intelligence

**Capability ID:** CAP-08  
**Parent:** [00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md](./00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md)

---

## Purpose

**Continuously understand operational state** — synthesize readiness, execution, workforce, issues, and supply into shared awareness that drives decisions **now**.

Intelligence is the **living picture** of the operation. It is not reporting. It is not a chatbot. It is **situational understanding**.

---

## Operational promise

Leaders and supervisors **never hunt** for information the organization already has. They **orient in seconds**, **prioritize by impact**, and **see change** — with optional AI partnership that speaks operational language.

---

## Inputs

- **Readiness states** — all locations, all active operations.
- **Execution phase and milestones** — what is live, what is late.
- **Workforce coverage** — gaps, call-downs, overrides.
- **Open issues and recoveries** — severity, age, owner.
- **Supply sufficiency** — critical shorts.
- **Asset status** — critical path equipment.
- **Knowledge** — retrieved for context on demand.
- **Historical patterns** — from Analytics (lightweight on floor, deeper for managers).

---

## Outputs

### Awareness

Unified **now and next** picture — site, cluster, or org scope.

### Readiness synthesis

Roll-up from inputs — not manual assembly.

### Risk

**What will fail next if untouched** — time-sensitive prioritization.

### Health

**Is service actually working** — outcome-oriented, not form-oriented.

### Predictions (maturity)

Pattern-based — chronic late handoff, repeat warmer fault before lunch.

### Operational summaries

Morning brief, shift change brief, transition brief.

### Manager guidance

Suggested walk order, escalation reminders, "what changed since yesterday."

### AI partnership (experiential)

Answers like: "How is breakfast going?" "What should I worry about?" "Which units need me first?" — grounded in live state, not generic chat.

## Operational rules

1. **Freshness** — stale intelligence is dangerous; show age or change.
2. **Scope by role** — supervisor sees cluster; manager sees site.
3. **Honest states** — never summarize healthy when blocked.
4. **One primary risk** on open — clarity first.
5. **AI cites grounding** — because sanitizer failed, not mystery.
6. **Floor simplicity** — intelligence concentrates at supervisor+.

## Success criteria

| Criterion | Measure |
|-----------|---------|
| **15-second orient** | Manager knows readiness and top risk |
| **Shared picture** | Supervisor and manager aligned |
| **Change visibility** | Delta since last visit/yesterday |
| **Actionable output** | Every risk suggests decision type |
| **Trust** | Matches walk reality |
