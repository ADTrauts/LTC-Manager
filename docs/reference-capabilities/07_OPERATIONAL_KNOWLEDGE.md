# Capability: Operational Knowledge

**Capability ID:** CAP-07  
**Parent:** [00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md](./00_REFERENCE_CAPABILITIES_INDEX.md)

---

## Purpose

**Preserve and deliver institutional knowledge** at the moment of work — so operations do not depend on who happened to be on shift.

Knowledge capability emulates **experienced staff teaching new staff** — situational, brief, attached to the work.

---

## Operational promise

Procedures, quirks, lessons, and policy references appear **where and when needed** — on the operation, location, equipment, issue, or task — not lost in binders or wikis nobody reads on the floor.

---

## Inputs

- **SOPs and procedures** — how to perform recurring work.
- **Photos and video** — visual setup, equipment controls, correct configuration.
- **Lessons learned** — from closed issues, surveys, near-misses.
- **Equipment knowledge** — quirks, reset steps, vendor notes.
- **Location knowledge** — what is different about this place.
- **Operation knowledge** — what always goes wrong on holiday brunch, fire drill protocol.
- **Policy documents** — handbook, regulatory reference — linked at decision moments.
- **Training content** — onboarding paths for location or role.
- **Tribal capture** — supervisor notes from floor.

---

## Outputs

- **Contextual knowledge delivery** — right content for current work moment.
- **Knowledge artifacts** — maintained, versioned, attributable.
- **Training completion record** (where required) — industry pack depth.
- **Search and retrieval** — find by operation, location, asset, issue.
- **AI-ready knowledge** — structured for operational partner retrieval (see Operational Intelligence).

---

## Knowledge attachment points

| Attached to | Examples |
|-------------|----------|
| **Operations** | Holiday brunch checklist, survey day priorities |
| **Locations** | "Warmer runs hot — set to 4" |
| **Equipment** | Reset sequence, last three failures |
| **Issues** | What fixed it last time |
| **Tasks / checks** | Inline instructions on compliance step |
| **Roles** | First day at servery guide |

**Rule:** Knowledge not attached to work is **secondary** — library, not homepage.

---

## Training

Training is **knowledge staged over time**:

- New hire at location — progressive disclosure.
- Cross-coverage floater — burst briefing for today's assignment.
- Refresher after incident — tied to issue lesson.

Training completes the loop between **Knowledge** and **Execution** — not separate LMS unless industry requires.

---

## AI retrieval

Operational Intelligence and AI partners **retrieve** knowledge by context:

- "What did we do last time warmer failed on 3B?"
- "How do we open this servery on survey day?"

Knowledge capability **feeds** intelligence — structured, grounded, maintained.

## Operational rules

1. **Brevity on floor** — three sentences beat three pages at point of use.
2. **Show today's delta** — changes beat static manual.
3. **Maintain on close** — issue resolution prompts lesson capture.
4. **Policy at decision** — handbook when discipline/compliance requires.
5. **Industry packs seed** — baseline SOPs; sites customize.
6. **No knowledge gate** — do not block work for unread manual unless policy mandates.
