# Operational Knowledge

**Audience:** All roles — knowledge is experienced in context  
**Status:** Canonical knowledge experience  
**Parent:** [00_REFERENCE_UX_INDEX.md](./00_REFERENCE_UX_INDEX.md)

---

## Purpose

Operational knowledge is **how work is done here** — not documentation for its own sake.

The platform must emulate how **experienced employees teach new ones**: short, situational, attached to the thing being worked, surfaced at the moment of need.

Knowledge does **not** live primarily in a document library. Libraries are where knowledge goes to die on the floor.

---

## User mindset

**New employee:** "What do I do — and what is different about *this* place?"

**Experienced employee:** "I know the job — remind me of the quirk."

**Supervisor:** "Don't let floaters fail because they weren't briefed."

**Manager:** "Procedures exist — I need them followed, not buried."

Everyone wants **answers at the point of work**, not search.

---

## Where knowledge attaches

Knowledge appears **on the thing being worked**, not floating alone.

### Operations

- What always goes wrong on holiday brunch.
- How this site runs fire drill during meal service.
- Handoff checklist between production and distribution.

**Experience:** Opening an operation shows **operation notes** — today's addendum + standing lessons.

---

### Locations

- "This servery's warmer runs hot."
- "Don't block the nursing station when staging."
- "Retail here has no sink — sanitizer bucket only."

**Experience:** Unit Workspace shows **location knowledge** — brief, scannable, maintained by supervisors.

---

### Equipment

- Model quirks, reset steps, who to call.
- "Dish machine — wait 30 seconds before start."
- Last three issues and what fixed them.

**Experience:** When equipment matters for current work, **equipment notes** appear — not full asset encyclopedia.

---

### Issues

- What was tried.
- Who from plant responded last time.
- Workaround that worked until repair.

**Experience:** Issue history is **story of resolution** — teaches the next responder.

---

### Tasks and checks

- Why this check exists.
- How to read failure.
- Photo of correct setup.

**Experience:** Instructions on the check itself — expand if needed, never mandatory wall of text before start.

---

## How experienced employees teach (and platform emulates)

| Human teaching | Platform emulation |
|----------------|-------------------|
| "Watch me open this servery" | Milestone sequence on Unit Workspace |
| "That warmer is tricky" | Equipment note at location |
| "We always forget the sanitizer log here" | Location lesson from past failures |
| "Read the board" | Operation changes for today |
| "Ask Maria" | Supervisor contact + contextual help |
| "It's in the binder" | **Last resort** — policy PDF linked from discipline/compliance moment only |

Shadowing is **sequential and situational**. The platform favors **short context bursts**, not manuals.

---

## Knowledge types

| Type | Examples | When surfaced |
|------|----------|---------------|
| **Procedure** | Opening steps, isolation tray protocol | Starting related work |
| **Caution** | Burn risk, allergen cross-contact | Before risky step |
| **Quirk** | Equipment behavior | When equipment in use |
| **Lesson learned** | Survey miss last month | Same operation or location |
| **Policy reference** | Union rule, handbook section | Discipline or compliance moment |
| **Today's change** | Menu mod, construction detour | Operation open |

---

## Information hierarchy (knowledge)

1. **Today's change** — always visible when relevant.
2. **Procedure for current task** — on the task.
3. **Location and equipment notes** — when at location.
4. **Historical lessons** — when pattern repeats.
5. **Full policy document** — deliberate deep link only.

**Never:** require reading Tier 5 before Tier 1 work.

---

## Who maintains knowledge

| Role | Maintains |
|------|-----------|
| **Managers** | Operation-level standards, policy links |
| **Supervisors** | Location notes, equipment quirks, lessons |
| **Employees** | Issue updates, "what worked" |
| **Organization** | Industry pack baselines, shared SOP templates |

Knowledge rots if unmaintained. Experience should prompt **light updates** when issues close or surveys fail — capture while fresh.

---

## Knowledge and compliance

Compliance knowledge is **procedural** — why temps matter, what failed last time — not legal terror.

Failed checks surface **what to do next**, not only red X.

Policy documents appear when **discipline or compliance decision** requires reference — union handbook at discipline moment, not homepage wallpaper.

---

## UX principles (Operational Knowledge)

1. **Context first** — right place, right time, right task.
2. **Brevity** — three sentences beat three pages on the floor.
3. **Show differences** — what is special about *here*.
4. **Learn from issues** — resolution history is teaching material.
5. **No binder hunt** — if it matters now, it is here.
6. **Respect expertise** — experienced users see less, not more noise.
7. **Industry packs seed** — sites customize, not start empty.

## Anti-patterns

- Homepage of PDF links.
- Training gate before every shift.
- Same generic procedure for every location.
- Knowledge disconnected from failed operation — repeat surprises.
- Wiki search as primary help.
- Blaming "they didn't read the manual."
