## Approved creative foundation

- Public product name: **Vssyl**, spoken exactly like **“vessel.”**
- Four durable storytelling pillars: **People. Places. Work. Time.** These are what the platform connects, not four separate software products.
- Campaign sign-off for this film: **“A clearer way to run the day.”**
- A single consistent fictional, institutional facility is the recurring setting. Use neutral 2.5D/isometric environments with flat 2D information overlays and role-appropriate product footage.
- Lead with realistic physical operations and their human consequences. Show expected timing, actual work, disruption, authorized response, continuity and preserved history.
- Narration guides the film. The musical bed is quiet, lightly optimistic and subordinate to speech. Animated word reveals follow the spoken words.
- The original creative decision was approximately **75% compelling operational/brand story and 25% grounded scenario detail**. This is guidance for emphasis, **not** a mandatory screen-time, shot-count or visual-medium split.
- Food & Nutrition and Facility Plant Operations are established Department Products; EVS scenes may demonstrate the facility's conceptual breadth, but EVS is not a customer-available Product unless its release status changes.

## Authority and non-goals

Creative decisions in this folder do **not** modify platform architecture, grant product authority, establish marketing/legal rights, or prove a capability exists. For contradictions, the current [product constitution](../../product/01_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md), [canonical brand specification](../../product/12_VSSYL_BRAND.md), Department Product contracts and current code take precedence.

The canonical brand specification's primary positioning line remains **“Your operations. All together.”** The film's **“A clearer way to run the day”** is an approved **campaign tagline**, not a replacement for that canonical positioning. Likewise, the film's architectural illustration palette does not override the canonical app palette or Geist typography.

The production standard is independent of a particular AI tool. ChatGPT, Codex, Google AI Studio, HeyGen and video editing software are production tools; the signed-off script, picture master, source provenance and approval record are authoritative.

## Workflow for the next video

1. Copy the [production template](VIDEO_PRODUCTION_TEMPLATE.md); define viewer, single objective and product-claim boundary.
2. Build a human, time-bound operational story that explains why the product matters before demonstrating interface mechanics.
3. Reuse the actual approved facility artwork, characters and motion vocabulary; create and approve missing assets before animation.
4. Verify each represented action against the current Product and role authority; separate observed fact, human decision, Request, Issue, Work Order and outcome.
5. Approve script, storyboard and visual direction; produce a natural full narration before locking final shot timing.
6. Build picture and music around the approved narration. Review caption readability, mobile crop, audio balance and People/Places/Work/Time cue alignment where used.
7. Archive final master, narration, script, source project, art, captions, licenses and approval record in durable storage; update the catalog. Do not publish commercially until rights and accuracy are reviewed.
