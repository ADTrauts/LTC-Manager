## 2. Story purpose and artistic result

The film explains that an institutional service operation is a connected sequence of **people, places, work and time**, not merely a set of forms or isolated tickets. One day is followed from dawn through a real staffing and equipment disruption, a human-led response, breakfast closeout and the next meal period.

**Why the creative direction was approved:**

- Real human activity before the software appears: employees arriving, kitchen preparation, servers preparing stations and supervisors confirming coverage.
- A **7:30 AM** staged breakfast deadline establishes timing, not general urgency.
- A call-off affects coverage; the supervisor makes an explicit adjustment.
- An example **48°F** refrigerator reading triggers documentation and operational decisions, not magical automatic repair.
- A maintenance Request is sent to Plant and an authorized supervisor creates a separate Work Order.
- Morning work and milestone evidence remain available when the next operating cycle begins.
- The closing enlarges **People / Places / Work / Time** and reveals each in sync with the natural narration.

The artwork deliberately connects simplified isometric physical environments to readable flat 2D operational overlays. The soundtrack is optimistic but well beneath narration.

## 5. Product truth embedded in the story

The scenario is an **illustrative demonstration**. People, names, facility layout, specific configured service times, quantities and readings are staged examples, not records from an actual customer facility.

**Call-off:** coverage represents actual staffing/assignments in context. A supervisor chooses an eligible replacement; software does not automatically allocate a person simply because a call-off was entered.

**48°F refrigeration:** outside the example cooler's configured limit. The employee documents a reading and corrective response. A human assesses food safety and follows local procedures. Neither 48°F alone nor a log record determines whether food can be retained, used, or discarded.

**Maintenance:** Report/Request, Issue, Work Order, Asset condition and Return to service are separate source facts. A Work Order is created by an authorized actor as a distinct decision. Completing it is not equivalent to recovery. The refrigerator stays out of service in the story until an authorized person changes its status.

**History and time:** a confirmed breakfast milestone can remain in review/history while the next cycle and due responsibilities become visible. An unresolved maintenance condition carries forward rather than silently disappearing at meal-period rollover.

**Product labels and release:** Vssyl is the platform. Food & Nutrition and Facility Plant Operations are separate AVAILABLE Department Products per current canonical references. Environmental Services imagery, if present in a background, is not a claim that its customer Product is available. For future work verify against the [Product Constitution](../../product/01_PRODUCT_CONSTITUTION.md), [Plant](../../product/14_FACILITY_PLANT_OPERATIONS.md) and [EVS](../../evs/README.md).
