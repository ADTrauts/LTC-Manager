# Dietary V1 — Employee Tablet Guide

Shared-tablet frontline guide for the controlled Dietary pilot.

## Signing in by Quick PIN

1. Confirm the tablet shows your Facility.
2. Enter your **Quick PIN** (STAFF and LEAD_TEAM_MEMBER only).
3. Supervisors and Managers use password accounts — not Quick PIN for management.

## Confirming identity and Unit

- Check your name on the Unit Workspace.
- Confirm the Unit name matches the servery you are working.

## Reading Assignment

- Your **confirmed** Unit, duty, and responsibility window appear when the Supervisor has confirmed the plan.
- If you see “Assignment not confirmed. Check with your Supervisor.” — ask your Supervisor. This does **not** mean you are absent.

## Servery Ready

Record **Servery Ready** when the servery is ready for service for that meal.

## Meal Service Started

Record **Meal Service Started** when meal service actually begins. This is separate from Ready.

## Offline status

- Status may show Online, Offline, Synchronizing, or Conflict Review Required.
- Offline work is limited to authorized Unit Workspace actions already prepared on this tablet.

## Saved on this tablet

Commands saved offline remain on this tablet until synchronization succeeds.

## Synchronization

When connectivity returns, pending commands synchronize. Do not keep tapping the same action repeatedly.

## Reauthentication required

If told to sign in again:

1. Sign out if needed.
2. Sign in with Quick PIN again.
3. Ask a Supervisor if the session was revoked.

## Signing out

Always sign out when finished so the next Employee does not see your Assignment.

## What not to do

- Do not edit Assignments.
- Do not confirm or reopen plans.
- Do not resolve management conflicts.
- Do not share your PIN.
- Do not use another Employee’s session.
