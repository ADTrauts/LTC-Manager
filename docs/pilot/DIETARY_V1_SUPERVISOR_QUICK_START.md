# Dietary V1 — Supervisor Quick Start

Controlled pilot operating note. No automatic scheduling. Assignments are prepared manually.

## Start of day

1. Sign in with your **password** (Supervisors do not use Quick PIN for management).
2. Confirm Facility and Dietary department in the shell.
3. Open **Assignment Board** (`/staffing/assignments`).
4. Confirm the operational date (Facility calendar day).

## Entering call-offs

1. Use **Schedule View** / staffing call-down entry for the day.
2. Called-off Employees remain visible on the Assignment Board as unavailable.
3. Call-off does **not** erase a prior Assignment — coverage becomes At Risk / Uncovered until replaced.

## Reviewing available staff

- Scheduled Employees appear in the Assignment pool.
- Called-off Employees remain visible but unavailable.
- Unscheduled Employees may be added only with an explicit source and reason.

## Preparing Assignments

1. Create Assignment: Employee, role/duty, Unit, responsibility window (Facility local time).
2. Sequential non-overlapping windows for one Employee are allowed.
3. Overlapping windows for one Employee are rejected.
4. Use **Add Coverage / Reassign** for replacements; reason required.

## Reviewing coverage

Coverage chips use: Covered · At Risk · Uncovered · Not Yet Assigned · Not Confirmed · Not Applicable.

Coverage is staffing risk — not proof meal service succeeded.

## Confirming the plan

1. Review remaining gaps.
2. Check **Acknowledge remaining coverage risks** when confirming with gaps.
3. **Confirm plan** — records actor and time. Employees then see confirmed Assignments.

## Reopening and overrides

- **Reopen** requires a reason.
- After confirmation, edits and replacements require a reason and append history.

## Monitoring Ready and Service Started

- Open each Unit Workspace for Servery Ready and Meal Service Started.
- Ready and Service Started are separate milestones.
- Staffing coverage and meal timing remain separate signals.

## Resolving offline conflicts

1. When a tablet shows Conflict Review Required, reconnect online.
2. Authorized Supervisors resolve conflicts on the Unit Workspace.
3. Original and resolution history remain.

## End-of-day review

1. Confirm late and Not Confirmed Units with GM / Managers.
2. Leave the plan CONFIRMED (formal CLOSED is not required for this pilot).
3. Next operational date does not inherit active Assignments incorrectly — navigate by date.

## Manual fallback

If the application is unavailable: use the paper continuity procedure in `DIETARY_V1_FALLBACK_AND_RECOVERY.md`, then reconcile online when restored.
