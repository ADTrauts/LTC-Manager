# Dietary Work — Employee Guide

Your Job Flow shows the primary current Work step and a small upcoming queue.

## Completing Work

- Confirm the step when done (explicit confirmation), or complete linked Evidence when the step requires it.
- Past due shows as **Past due — not confirmed**. That does not mean you failed — it means confirmation was not recorded.
- Open the linked Procedure for guidance. Reading it does not mark the step complete.

## Offline

On a bound tablet, Work completion can be saved on this tablet and synchronized later. Conflicts (for example reassignment while offline) go to conflict review — do not invent a second completion.
