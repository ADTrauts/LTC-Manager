# Visual 3 — Proposed People & Coverage layout

**Proposed.** Not built. Illustrative names, not facility data.

Open the [wireframe](./03_proposed_layout.html).

One page, three regions, in this order:

1. **Today** — read-only. Required versus assigned for the current service date, from `buildDietaryCoverageSummary`. A Department with no published requirements says Coverage is not configured.
2. **Coverage requirements** — the editor that already exists. This is the only region that saves.
3. **Organization** — read-only counts, Teams, lead, people with no Team, and qualification counts. Edit links go to Employee Builder.

Tabs were considered and not recommended. The unconfigured state and a shortage need to be visible together, and People & Coverage is already one Department Builder tab. A 100-person roster does not belong in region 3.

Region 2 stays a drawer for one expectation: location, period, role, count, draft, and effective date. Group the closed list by room so seventeen serveries are scannable.
