# Dietary Log Book Guide

Historical Operational Evidence search for Dietary (Phase 9C).

## Open

`/staffing/log-book` (Supervisor+ password). Detail: `/staffing/log-book/[recordId]`.

## Filters

Operational date range, Unit, Asset id, Template key, Corrective-action only.

## Each result shows

Template name and version, date/window, Unit/Asset, recorder label, status, occurrence/recorded/sync times, offline/correction indicators.

## Record detail

Read-only values, corrective action, append-preserving correction history, and the Template snapshot preserved at submit. Later Template edits do not rewrite the record. Use the browser Print dialog for a printable view.

## Authority

Department-scoped. STAFF do not get Department-wide Log Book. Quick PIN does not grant Log Book. FA without Dietary operational relationship is denied.

## Claims

Records do not claim regulatory compliance merely because they exist. Missing evidence means “Record Not Submitted,” not that the underlying check did not occur.
