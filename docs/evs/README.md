# Environmental Services

**Status — AVAILABLE** as of November 24, 2026 in the [Product registry](../../src/lib/department-products/registry.ts) (`versionLabel` `1.0`). Customer browse, purchase (via the existing department-count path), install and operation are permitted for entitled facilities. This release does not add a dedicated billing SKU.

The Product blueprint is [17 — Environmental Services](../product/17_ENVIRONMENTAL_SERVICES.md). Implemented building blocks include [EVS Rhythm starter drafts](../../src/lib/operational-cycles/evs-starter.ts) with Opening, Cleaning and Closeout Phases and Shift Opened and Area Ready Key Points, explicit EVS qualifications through the [operational role registry](../../src/lib/scheduling/assignment-roles/index.ts), and shared Workforce Assignment, employee Job Flow, Records and Assets. Assignment zones were retired on November 23, 2026. The [EVS browser spec](../../tests/evs-browser/ci-gate.spec.ts) covers Records, Asset Issues and role/Department boundaries. The [Assignment spec](../../tests/evs-assignment-browser/ci-gate.spec.ts) covers explicit multi-room placement, offline scope and cross-Facility rejection.

The blueprint and the [Dashboard contribution declaration](../dashboard/DEPARTMENT_DASHBOARD_CONTRACT.md#dashboard-contribution-declarations) were completed on November 23, 2026, with gate tests. Room turnover, isolation workflows and bed readiness remain out of V1 scope: role labels such as `DISCHARGE_CLEAN` and `ISOLATION_CLEAN` express assignment eligibility only. Generic Work instructions in the older EVS guidebooks are superseded. The [status register](../DOCUMENTATION_STATUS.md) identifies the older files.
