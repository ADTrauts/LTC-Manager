# 07 — Operational Visibility

## Visibility equation

Operational data is visible only when all applicable predicates pass:

```text
facility tenancy
AND role/action authorization
AND principal location access
AND active department projection includes the physical target
AND projected experience includes the domain
AND domain record ownership/scope matches
```

Capabilities provide operational relevance. They are not sufficient by themselves.

## Domain rules

### Meal service and meal times

Required:

- Dietary projection;
- `MEAL_SERVICE`;
- applicable room or legacy Unit;
- record linked to the projected Unit/Room context.

Never visible in EVS or Plant projections.

### Service logs and food safety

Required:

- owning department matches;
- `SERVICE_LOGS` or `FOOD_SAFETY`;
- target physical scope is projected.

Generic log routes must not imply that every department sees every log template or submission.

### Cleaning and room status

Required:

- owning department matches;
- `CLEANING` for cleaning work;
- `ROOM_STATUS` for EVS room-state controls;
- projected Room or compatibility Unit.

Dietary cleaning can exist without exposing EVS room-state data because experience and ownership are evaluated together.

### Assets and department equipment

Required:

- `ASSET_MANAGEMENT`;
- asset physical link is inside the projection domain scope;
- department ownership or experience rule matches.

Dietary may receive a Dietary equipment experience. This does not expose Plant PM schedules or unrestricted Plant asset controls.

### Preventive maintenance

Required:

- Plant projection;
- facility Plant maintenance policy or `BUILDING_MAINTENANCE`;
- asset/space is inside the resulting policy scope.

Never visible in Dietary or EVS projections solely because the asset occupies their room.

### Repairs, issues, and work orders

Required:

- `REPAIRS` or the mapped maintenance experience;
- responsible/requesting department rule;
- target inside projected location scope.

Cross-department issue awareness must be an explicit experience rule, such as “requesting department may see its submitted issue status.” It must not be inferred from shared physical location.

### Inspections

Required:

- `INSPECTIONS`;
- inspection definition/occurrence owner matches the active department, unless explicitly cross-department;
- physical target is projected.

### Knowledge

Required:

- `KNOWLEDGE`;
- article department scope matches;
- any location association is inside the projection.

Facility-wide articles require explicit facility scope and must remain labeled.

### Work queue and assignments

Required:

- `WORK_QUEUE` or an experience-specific work capability;
- assignment department matches;
- assignment target is inside projection scope;
- employee access further narrows results.

Assignment does not broaden the projection.

### Readiness

Required:

- node exists in the department projection;
- profile matches the active department;
- only projected domain signals are evaluated.

Structural ancestor readiness is aggregated from projected descendants.

## Loader contract

Every domain loader migrated to projection accepts a scope generated by the engine:

```ts
loadDomainRecords({
  facilityId,
  departmentId,
  scope: projection.queryScope.domains.get(domain),
});
```

The loader:

- validates facility tenancy;
- constrains Unit/Room IDs in the database query;
- applies department ownership predicates;
- returns no data when scope is absent;
- never accepts client-supplied capability arrays.

Filtering only after loading is insufficient for security, performance, and accidental cross-department composition.

## Composition contract

Composition receives:

- allowed experience descriptors;
- already-scoped domain data;
- projected readiness and ancestry.

It may order, summarize, or omit allowed content for the surface. It may not restore a domain absent from the projection.

## Navigation contract

Navigation uses experiences to advertise reachable destinations. It does not authorize those destinations and does not filter data.

## Facility Overview

Facility mode composes department-scoped result sets. A record is never stripped of department ownership to make a global list.

Cross-department summaries should use stable grouping:

```text
physical location → department → domain signal
```

This permits leadership correlation without creating a universal operational scope for ordinary department users.

## Deny-by-default behavior

- no projection domain scope: loader returns empty;
- inactive department: empty projection;
- unknown capability: no experience;
- missing ownership metadata: exclude from department mode unless a certified compatibility rule exists;
- policy load failure: do not grant policy scope;
- mismatched target facility: reject.

Compatibility exceptions must be named, tested, measured, and removable. They must not become generic “empty means all” behavior.

## Observability

Track:

- projection size by department and facility;
- excluded staged/undesignated counts;
- unknown capabilities;
- records excluded for missing department ownership;
- direct-link projection denials;
- compatibility adapter usage;
- divergence between legacy and shadow projection outputs during migration.

Operational logs must contain IDs and rule codes, not resident-sensitive content.
